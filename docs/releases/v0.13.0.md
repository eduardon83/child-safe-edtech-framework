# CSEF v0.13.0: labels and metadata

Quality labels and catalogues ask for the same facts in different forms. CSEF already holds most of them in
`csef.yaml` and in its scan results. v0.13 turns them into a label package: describe the product once, submit
it to any label that asks. Guide: `framework/07-labels-and-metadata.md`.

**Serious-game pre-check** (`tooling/precheck.py`)

- 22 quality criteria for serious games (Caserman et al. 2020), in three groups: the serious part, the game
  part, and the balance between them. They are one of the two publications behind the quality label of the
  Serious Games Gütegemeinschaft.
- Each criterion is mapped to CSEF rules and Compass targets (`framework/serious-games/criteria.yaml`):
  5 covered by CSEF, 10 in part, 5 that need a team statement, and 2 where CSEF sets limits for children.
- Statements with evidence go in a new `serious_game` block in csef.yaml. Each criterion gets a status: ready,
  partial, missing, or CSEF issue.
- Where the criteria recommend high scores, competition or emotional hooks, the CSEF rule applies for
  under-18s.
- The pre-check is preparation, not a label. The label's own test rules are not public.

**Metadata export** (`tooling/metadata.py`)

- A new `metadata` block in csef.yaml.
- SG-MDF (Serious Games Metadata Format, DIN SPEC 91380) Core fields and the learning-games profile, as JSON
  and XML. The DIN SPEC defines the format as a grammar with no official schema, so the files say they are
  CSEF's serialisation. CSEF copies no text or value lists from the DIN SPEC.
- schema.org LearningResource (LRMI) JSON-LD for any learning product.
- Title, summary, age groups, game mode, purchases and the required data statement come from what csef.yaml
  already declares.

**Label package** (`tooling/metadata.py --package`)

- One zip: metadata, CSEF findings, the pre-check, an evidence index and MANIFEST.json with SHA-256 hashes.
- No learner data. `--include-evidence` copies local evidence files; read them first.

**Numbers.** 124 rules (unchanged). 83 sources (R-CASERMAN2020, DIN-SPEC-91380 added), plus 9 in the Portugal
pack.
