# CSES v0.4.0: KIDS Act commercial practices and AI chatbots

Eight new rules from the EU KIDS Act proposal (COM(2026) 681), with AI now covered in more depth.

## Commercial practices (Recital 31, Article 13)

- **CORE-COM-05** Purchases are labelled as purchases, in real time, in the national currency.
- **CORE-COM-06** No design for impulsive or unwanted spending: no one-tap or in-content purchases, friction between content and buying.
- **CORE-COM-07** No disguised advertising: sponsorship, product placement and brand partnerships clearly identified (Level 1, Unfair Commercial Practices Directive Annex I point 11).
- **CORE-COM-08** No commercial content inside learning activities for children (Level 3).

## AI companions and chatbots (Recitals 32 to 34, Article 14)

- **CORE-AI-08** Conversation memory is off by default.
- **CORE-AI-09** AI is re-tested regularly and monitored after release (CSES applies this to every provider, although the proposal exempts micro and small enterprises from post-market monitoring).
- **CORE-AI-10** An AI assistant inside a game or app is opt-in, not prominent, and easy to turn off.
- **CORE-AI-11** AI never nudges children to spend or towards ads.

Also: the age assurance rule now cites Article 28(1) and Article 31.

Recitals explain intent; articles carry the obligations. CSES rules cite both. Article 13 binds social and
video services and AI chatbots; CSES applies it to games and learning apps at Level 2 as good practice.
