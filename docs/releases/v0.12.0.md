# CSEF v0.12.0: in-game purchases, as EU consumer authorities read the law

In March 2025 the EU consumer protection authorities (the CPC Network) published Key Principles on in-game
virtual currencies. In September 2026 they adopted a Joint Statement on video games and opened coordinated
actions against nine game companies. They found that many companies had not changed. These texts are not law,
but they say how the authorities read the law they enforce: the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, the
Consumer Rights Directive and the Unfair Contract Terms Directive. v0.12 maps them into CSEF.

**New sources**

- CPC-VC: Key Principles on In-game Virtual Currencies (21 March 2025).
- CPC-JS: Joint Statement on Stronger Consumer Protection in Video Games (30 September 2026).
- CRD: Directive 2011/83/EU, Consumer Rights Directive.
- UCTD: Directive 93/13/EEC on unfair terms in consumer contracts.

**New rules**

- **CORE-COM-09** (MUST, tier 1, feature `purchases`) Before any purchase the buyer sees what is bought, who
  sells it, the real price, withdrawal rights, the legal guarantee, payment and delivery. Withdrawal works for
  14 days, unused currency included. The digital-content waiver is a separate, express step, confirmed afterwards.
- **CORE-COM-10** (MUST, tier 1, feature `purchases`) Terms are plain and fair. No one-sided changes to bought
  content or to the currency's value. Bans and suspensions come with a reason and a way to contest them.

**Changed rules**

- **A-ECO-03** Real-world price next to every item and currency amount, without discounts; one purchasable
  currency, no exchanges; exact amounts, not only bundles; spending off for children unless a guardian approves.
  Now also applies to profile B.
- **CORE-COM-04** Also covers urging children to ask adults to buy, products likely to appeal to children, age
  gates that do not remove the duty, and spending off by default with working parental controls.
- CORE-COM-03 (countdown timers, false scarcity), CORE-COM-05, CORE-COM-06 (purchase prompts apart from
  gameplay, models built on heavy spenders) and A-ECO-01 (loot boxes) cite the CPC texts. So does Compass TRN-04.

**Digital Fairness Act**

The source now points to the official proposal, COM(2026) 1100, adopted on 11 November 2026. Summaries of the
official text confirm the points CSEF took from the leaked draft. Article numbers are still to be checked on
EUR-Lex (watch list).

**Detection**

- CORE-COM-09: "all sales are final", "non-refundable", "no refunds" in shop copy (medium confidence).
- CORE-COM-10: "we reserve the right to change or remove virtual currency" in terms (medium confidence).

**Numbers.** 124 rules (2 added). 81 sources (4 added), plus 9 in the Portugal pack.
