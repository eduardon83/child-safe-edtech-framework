# CSEF v0.14.1: AI Act guidance

A small update. Since v0.13 the Commission has published practical guidance on the AI Act. CSEF now cites it,
and two AI rules say more clearly what a product must do.

**New sources**

- Guidelines on prohibited AI practices (4 Feb 2025). Not binding. Examples for Art. 5, including emotion
  recognition in education and exploiting age-related vulnerability. Cited by CORE-AI-01, CORE-AI-02, B-EVL-02.
- Guidelines on the AI system definition (6 Feb 2025). Not binding. Helps decide whether the AI Act applies at all.
- Guidelines on transparency obligations under Art. 50 (20 Jul 2026).
- Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content (June 2026), with the Commission opinion of 9 Jul 2026
  and the AI Board adequacy assessment. Voluntary; signing it is one way to show compliance with Art. 50(2), (4)
  and (5).
- Commission report on the review of prohibitions and high-risk AI (22 May 2026). On the watch list.

**Changed rules**

- CORE-AI-03 (AI is disclosed). Covers chatbots, AI agents and AI avatars or characters. Children are told from
  the first interaction, clearly and accessibly. For children, CSEF assumes AI is never "obvious". Generated
  audio, images, video and text carry a machine-readable mark; products already on the market before
  2 Aug 2026 have until 2 Dec 2026. Realistic AI media has a visible label at first view; in a story or game it
  must not break the experience.
- CORE-AI-12 (no real people in generation). The AI Omnibus ban on AI that generates non-consensual intimate
  content or child sexual abuse material, including nudification apps, applies from December 2026.

**Dates**

- Annex III high-risk rules (education included): 2 Dec 2027.
- Annex I high-risk rules (AI in regulated products, for example toys): 2 Aug 2028.

**Watch list**

- Art. 50 guidance is published and cited.
- Still pending: guidelines on high-risk classification and the standards for high-risk systems; updated
  prohibitions guidelines covering prohibition 9; the next annual review of Art. 5 and Annex III.

Rules: 131. Sources: 94, plus 9 in the Portugal pack.
