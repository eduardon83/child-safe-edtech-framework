# CSES v0.2.0: company packs and Pack Builder

Each organisation can now write its own child-safety policy on top of the public standard, and every
product is checked against both in one run.

## New

- **Company packs** with a `policy` section:
  - `minimum_level`: every product is assessed at least at this level
  - `no_go`: standard rules your organisation never breaks; they block at any level and confidence
  - `forbid_dependencies` and `forbid_phrases`: packages you never ship, copy you never show children
  - `require_decision_for_features`: sensitive features (AI, streaks, chat...) need a decision record
  - `review_every_days` and `require_agent_instructions`
- **Pack Builder**: build your pack in a web form and publish it to your GitHub repository.
  https://eduardon83.github.io/child-safe-edtech-framework/pack-builder/
- `python3 tooling/build.py --validate-pack your-pack.yaml`
- Example: `examples/company-packs/acme.yaml`

Policy findings cite a source of type `policy`, so reports separate your organisation's choices from law.

## Upgrade

Pin the action to `@v0.2.0`. Existing manifests and packs keep working unchanged.
