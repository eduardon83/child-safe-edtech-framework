# CSES v0.1.0: draft for public comment

The first public draft of the **Child-Safe EdTech Standard**: an open standard and toolkit that helps
EdTech teams build software children use for learning safely, and show it.

> Not legal advice and not a certification. CSES produces *pre-compliance* reports.

## What's in this release

- **89 rules** in a core set plus three profiles: **A** educational games, **B** game-based applications
  and software, **C** gamified applications, with age-band modifiers from 3 to 17.
- **Three compliance levels** each team chooses: **1** Legal baseline, **2** Forward-compliant (adds the
  EU KIDS Act and Digital Fairness Act proposals and the DSA Art. 28 guidelines), **3** Best practice
  (adds research and expert recommendations). Rules above the chosen level appear as "next level" items,
  never as failures.
- **50 sources** with type, status and verification date, cited on every rule and listed in every report.
- **Design for learning:** new rules on look, sound, motion and rewards, based on research gathered for
  the European Digital Education Hub game-based learning squad (seductive details, animation, emotional
  design, reward pile-up, intrinsic motivation).
- **Packs** to add national laws, sector rules, literature or company red lines without forking.
  Includes a Portugal pack (draft).
- **Scanner** (`tooling/scan.py`): writes a pre-compliance report, a prioritised improvement plan, JSON
  and SARIF.
- **`cses-review` skill for Claude**: runs the scanner, verifies findings, reviews what patterns cannot
  see, and finalises both documents. Download `cses-review.zip` below.
- **GitHub Action**, generated Semgrep rules, PR template, and templates for the manifest, DPIA, AI risk
  file, waivers and AI coding agent instructions.

## Getting started

```bash
cp templates/cses.yaml /path/to/your-product/cses.yaml   # describe your product
pip install pyyaml
python3 tooling/scan.py --repo /path/to/your-product --out-dir cses-report
```

Or add the check to your product's CI:

```yaml
- uses: eduardon83/child-safe-edtech-standards@v0.1.0
```

## Status and known limits

- The **EU KIDS Act** is a Commission proposal (COM(2026) 681). Feedback is open until 3 December 2026.
- **Digital Fairness Act** rules rely on a leaked draft and will be checked against the official text.
- **Portugal pack** entries marked "verify" need confirmation by a Portuguese legal reviewer.
- Static analysis finds signals, not proof. Several rules are human checks by design.

## Feedback wanted

Open an issue for false positives, missing patterns, rule wording, or sources. We especially welcome
**country packs** reviewed by a local lawyer or DPO, and evidence that supports or challenges the
Level 3 rules. See CONTRIBUTING.md and GOVERNANCE.md.
