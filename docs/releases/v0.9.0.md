# CSEF v0.9.0: Literacy Compass and personal questions

CSEF asked one question until now: does the product do harm? v0.9 adds a second: **what does the product teach
about digital, media and AI literacy, and how do we know?**

- 45 targets on 8 axes, mapped to DigComp 3.0, the EC-OECD AI Literacy Framework and UNESCO's media,
  information and AI frameworks.
- An evidence ladder per target (Stated, Designed, Tested, Measured), with the passages behind each level.
- Checks for unsupported learning claims and for copy that teaches the opposite (hidden curriculum).
- A compass rose with a needle to the next step, and a crosswalk so the same evidence answers the EU guidelines
  on digital education content, Swedish Edtech's Kvalitetsportfolio and UNICEF's EdTech for Good.
- A profile, never a score: no totals, no ranking, no badge.

**Personal questions.** Products increasingly ask children about their lives: "about me" profiles, mood
check-ins, onboarding surveys. v0.9 adds a `survey` feature, eight rules and six manifest checks so that every
question has a purpose and can be skipped, nothing is asked about other people or about origin, religion,
health or family situation (even indirectly), the right person answers for the child's age, teachers see
class totals only, and a disclosure reaches a trusted adult. The Portugal pack adds the DGE's MIME approval for
questionnaires in public schools. A register template is in `templates/survey-register.md`.

The two parts meet: evidence collected for the Compass follows the personal-question rules, and a new Compass
target (TRN-08) turns each question into a small lesson in the child's own data.

Try it: `python3 tooling/compass.py --input examples/compass/fact-quest --manifest examples/compass/fact-quest/csef.yaml`

Feedback is welcome in issues labelled `compass` or `survey`.
