# CSEF v0.9.0: Literacy Compass

CSEF asked one question until now: does the product do harm? v0.9 adds a second: **what does the product teach
about digital, media and AI literacy, and how do we know?**

- 44 targets on 8 axes, mapped to DigComp 3.0, the EC-OECD AI Literacy Framework and UNESCO's media,
  information and AI frameworks.
- An evidence ladder per target (Stated, Designed, Tested, Measured), with the passages behind each level.
- Checks for unsupported learning claims and for copy that teaches the opposite (hidden curriculum).
- A compass rose with a needle to the next step, and a crosswalk so the same evidence answers the EU guidelines
  on digital education content, Swedish Edtech's Kvalitetsportfolio and UNICEF's EdTech for Good.
- A profile, never a score: no totals, no ranking, no badge.

Try it: `python3 tooling/compass.py --input examples/compass/fact-quest --manifest examples/compass/fact-quest/csef.yaml`

Feedback on the axes, targets and evidence ladder is welcome in issues labelled `compass`.
