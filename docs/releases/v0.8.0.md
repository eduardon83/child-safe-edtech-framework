# v0.8.0: the CSEF brand

CSEF now has its own look: a house built from four blocks, one for each group that builds it (developers,
designers, educators and companies), with a child safe inside.

## What changed

- **Landing page** for people outside the development team: companies, designers, educators and developers
  each get a clear starting point. "Set your boundaries" leads to the questionnaire.
- **Questionnaire** redesigned: what you get, steps, progress and colours for red lines and approvals.
- **Every output** carries the logo and colours: staff policy, boundaries report, scan dashboard,
  code-of-conduct report, Pack Builder, PDF and Word. Light and dark mode follow the reader's settings.
- **Brand kit** in `brand/`: logo, icons, colours, fonts, GitHub banner and social preview.

No rule changes. Not legal advice and not a certification. CSEF is a framework, not a standard.
