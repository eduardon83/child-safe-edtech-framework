# CSES v0.3.0: dashboard, PDF and Word exports

Scan results can now be shared with people who don't read Markdown: product owners, school clients and
legal counsel.

## New

- **Interactive dashboard** (`dashboard.html`, self-contained, works offline):
  - status at each compliance level (Legal baseline, Forward-compliant, Best practice), with the chosen
    level and any policy minimum marked
  - the five age bands, highlighting the declared ones and their open issues
  - colour-coded findings grouped by rule, with filters by severity and level and a search box
  - a human-check list you can tick, annotate and download as CSV
  - the prioritised improvement plan
  - a **For legal counsel** section: legal scope questions, Level 1 rules to confirm, proposals relied on,
    waivers in force
  - all sources with type, status and verification date
  - light and dark mode, mobile layout, print stylesheet
- **PDF** (`report.pdf`) via Chrome/Chromium, falling back to WeasyPrint.
- **Word** (`report.docx`) with tables ready for comments and sign-off.

## How

```bash
python3 tooling/scan.py --repo . --out-dir cses-report --export html,pdf,docx
python3 tooling/export.py --input cses-report/findings.json --formats html,pdf,docx
```

GitHub Action: `export: html,pdf,docx` (default `html`). Pin to `@v0.3.0`.

`findings.json` moves to format 2; re-run the scanner before exporting older scans.
