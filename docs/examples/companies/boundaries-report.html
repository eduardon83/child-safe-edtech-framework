Notes

  • For these, approval adds a written check, but the framework rules already apply at your level and approval cannot switch them off: Streaks that children can lose; Notifications that bring children back; Public rankings of children; Generative AI that children talk to or that makes content for them; Using learning data for research or product studies.

Legal and recommended, side by side

Required by law, whatever you answer

These apply at every level. Your answer only decides whether the scanner also blocks weak signals.

  • Advertising or ad tracking shown to children (CORE-COM-01, CORE-COM-07): No advertising or ad tracking SDKs
  • Pressure design (countdowns, fake scarcity, guilt messages, pre-ticked boxes) (CORE-COM-03): No dark patterns
  • Characters without AI that act like a friend (mascots, guides, scripted companions) (CORE-COM-03): No dark patterns
  • Contact from people outside the child's class or group (CORE-SAF-05): No contact from strangers; play stays in closed groups
  • Playing with children from other classes or schools (CORE-SAF-05): No contact from strangers; play stays in closed groups
  • Emotion recognition (AI reading feelings from faces, voice or behaviour) (CORE-AI-01): No emotion recognition on learners
  • Generative AI that children talk to or that makes content for them (CORE-AI-03, CORE-AI-05, CORE-AI-09, CORE-AI-10): AI interaction and AI-generated content are disclosed
  • Training AI models on children's data (CORE-AI-04): AI inventory and no training on learners' data by default
  • Face, voice or fingerprint identification, webcam proctoring (CORE-DAT-08, B-EVL-02): No biometric identification of minors
  • Sharing children's data with a company without a signed agreement (CORE-DAT-02): Controller and processor roles are defined, with processor agreements
  • Children's data stored or processed outside Europe (CORE-DAT-03): No transfers outside the EEA without a valid mechanism
  • Real children's data in development, testing or demos (CORE-DAT-07): No real learner data outside production
  • Session recording, heatmaps or third-party analytics on child screens (CORE-COM-02, CORE-DAT-04): Third-party analytics and session recording reviewed
  • Using learning data for research or product studies (B-DAT-01): Research use of learning data is separated
  • Learner data readable without access rules (e.g. open database tables) (CORE-SEC-02): Access control on every store of learner data
