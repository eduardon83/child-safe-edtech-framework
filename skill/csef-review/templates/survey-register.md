## Checks before adding a question

- [ ] The purpose is about learning, and the product works the same for a child who skips it.
- [ ] It asks nothing about other people by name, photo, voice or detail (CORE-DAT-14).
- [ ] It cannot reveal, even indirectly, origin, religion, health, disability, sexual orientation, political
      opinion, migration status, separation, bereavement, care, income or parents' jobs (CORE-DAT-15).
- [ ] Support needs are asked as settings (bigger text, read-aloud), not as a diagnosis.
- [ ] "Prefer not to say" has the same size, colour and position weight as the other answers.
- [ ] Answering earns nothing: no points, coins, badges or unlocks.
- [ ] Teachers see totals for at least 5 children per figure; uniform or small figures are hidden.
- [ ] If free text is allowed, the disclosure route agreed with the school is linked as `survey.safeguarding`.
- [ ] If answers are used for evaluation or research, the approval is linked as `survey.research_approval`
      (ethics, legal basis and, in Portugal, EduQA approval through MIME for questionnaires in public schools).
- [ ] The question and its purpose are covered by the DPIA.

## Child-facing explanation

Write, for each age band, the one or two sentences the child sees before the questions: why we ask, who sees
the answers, and that skipping is fine. Test them with children of that age.
