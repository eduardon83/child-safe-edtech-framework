# DPIA starter for products used by children

A **Data Protection Impact Assessment** (GDPR Art. 35) is a written risk assessment done *before*
processing that is likely to result in a high risk to people's rights. Products used by children at
scale, with AI, or that monitor learners usually need one.

**Who signs it?** The controller. In school deployments that is usually the school, school group or
Ministry; the provider (processor) supplies the technical content. When the provider sells directly to
families, the provider is the controller.

This starter is not a substitute for your DPO or your national authority's template. Check whether your
authority publishes a list of processing that always needs a DPIA (see the national pack).

## Part B. Necessity, proportionality and risk

1. Is each data category necessary for its purpose? What could you drop or pseudonymise?
2. How are learners, guardians and teachers informed? Is there a child-friendly version?
3. How are data subject rights executed (access, erasure, export)?
