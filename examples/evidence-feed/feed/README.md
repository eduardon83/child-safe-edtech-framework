# Open evidence feed: Garden of Numbers

Group figures from play, in the csef-evidence-0.1 format of the Child-Safe EdTech Framework (CSEF). They describe how groups of pupils played, never one pupil or one class.

| | |
|---|---|
| Game | Garden of Numbers (https://example.org/garden-of-numbers) |
| Maker | Example Studio (https://example.org) |
| Period | 2026-09 to 2026-11 |
| Players (rounded to 5) | 60 in 6 classes |
| Age bands | B2 |
| Countries | PT |
| Collected | in the maker’s own study |
| Thresholds | at least 10 players and 3 classes behind every figure; 1 small cell(s) removed |
| Licence | CC0-1.0 |

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| population_id | The group: 'dataset' is everyone in this file. |
| unit_id | A unit of play (level, mission); * is the whole game. |
| players | Players behind the row, rounded to 5. |
| classes | Classes behind the row. |
| ev_time_p25_min | EV-TIME: Active minutes from start to finish of the unit, among players who finished: 25th, 50th and 75th percentile. Gaps over 60 seconds count as 60. |
| ev_time_p50_min | EV-TIME: Active minutes from start to finish of the unit, among players who finished: 25th, 50th and 75th percentile. Gaps over 60 seconds count as 60. |
| ev_time_p75_min | EV-TIME: Active minutes from start to finish of the unit, among players who finished: 25th, 50th and 75th percentile. Gaps over 60 seconds count as 60. |
| ev_done_share | EV-DONE: Share of players who started the unit and finished it. |
| ev_stuck_share | EV-STUCK: Share of players a detector marked as stuck in the unit. |
| ev_help_share | EV-HELP: Share of players who used a hint or help in the unit. |
| ev_gain_mean | EV-GAIN: Mean change between the pre and post check (score / max), for players with both. The instrument is named in META.json. |
| ev_gain_n | EV-GAIN: Mean change between the pre and post check (score / max), for players with both. The instrument is named in META.json. |
| ev_access_captions | EV-ACCESS: Share of players who turned on each declared accessibility setting. |
| ev_access_reduced_motion | EV-ACCESS: Share of players who turned on each declared accessibility setting. |
| ev_access_text_size | EV-ACCESS: Share of players who turned on each declared accessibility setting. |
| ev_stop_share | EV-STOP: Share of sessions that ended at a natural stopping point. |
| ev_mode_solo | EV-MODE: Share of sessions played solo, in teams or teacher-led. |
| ev_mode_teacher_led | EV-MODE: Share of sessions played solo, in teams or teacher-led. |
| ev_mode_team | EV-MODE: Share of sessions played solo, in teams or teacher-led. |
| ev_device_desktop | EV-DEVICE: Share of sessions per device class. |
| ev_device_laptop | EV-DEVICE: Share of sessions per device class. |
| ev_device_tablet | EV-DEVICE: Share of sessions per device class. |

Learning gain instrument: Fractions check, 10 items (pre and post).

## How to cite

Example Studio (2026). Open evidence feed for Garden of Numbers, 2026-09 to 2026-11. csef-evidence-0.1.

## Not in this file

No player codes, sessions, timestamps, schools, classes or free text. No retention, daily users, streaks or revenue.
Figures are the maker's own unless a researcher or curator has checked the method.
