# Research agreement (fictional example)

Between Example Studio (controller for the study) and Example University (research partner).

- **Purpose:** learn which fraction tasks pupils aged 6 to 9 find hard, and whether the game helps them compare fractions.
- **Classes:** only classes whose school, teacher and families agreed. They get the study build (version branch `study`).
- **Data:** events listed in docs/event-catalogue.yaml. No names, no free text, no device fingerprints.
- **Extracts for the university:** relative time, no time zone, no key. Example Studio keeps the link between player codes and accounts.
- **Ethics approval:** Example University ethics committee, ref. 2026-031.
- **Open release:** group figures only, as a csef-evidence-0.1 feed, at least 10 players and 3 classes per figure.
- **Leaving the study:** a family can withdraw at any time; the pupil keeps playing the normal build.
- **Retention:** study events deleted 12 months after the end of the study.
