# Fact Quest: game design document

## 1. Learning goals

Fact Quest is a detective game. Each case has learning goals mapped to DigComp 3.0 and the AILit framework.

- Case 1 "The Viral Photo": learners will check who published a photo and compare it with two other sources before deciding how far to trust it (DigComp 1.2, lateral reading).
- Case 2 "The Search Race": players must search with a limited number of queries and choose the best source for the question (DigComp 1.1).
- Case 3 "Sage Gets It Wrong": learners evaluate answers from the AI helper and decide to accept, revise or reject them, giving a reason (AILit Engage 3).
- Case 4 "Real or Generated?": players spot AI-generated images and manipulated media and learn to check provenance (AILit Create 4).

Ages: 10 to 12 play cases 1 to 3 at basic level; 13 to 15 play all cases at intermediate level.

## 2. Mechanics

The trust meter replaces right or wrong answers: learners set a trust level and must give one reason. A debrief screen at the end of each case shows which checks the learner used.

## 3. The AI helper

Sage is an AI helper built on a language model. Sage is labelled as AI on every screen and tells players at the start that it predicts words from patterns in training data, so it can make mistakes. In Case 3 Sage gives some wrong answers on purpose.

## 4. Shop

Players can buy cosmetic hats for the detective with gems. Gems are bought in the app store.

## 5. Accessibility

All case texts have read-aloud, and a dyslexia-friendly font is available. Clues are given as text and images.
