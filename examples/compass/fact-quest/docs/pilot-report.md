# Pilot report, spring 2026

We piloted Case 1 and Case 3 with 3 classes (68 pupils, ages 11 to 13) and 4 teachers in two schools. Teachers tested the debrief questions.

Learners who checked a second source before setting the trust meter rose from 22% in the first round to 61% in the third round. Teachers said the AI helper's planted mistakes started the best discussions on evaluating AI answers.

Next step: a comparison group study with a university partner.
