# Example Learning Games Alliance: Code of Conduct (fictional sample)

This is a fictional code written to test the CSEF code-of-conduct comparison. It contains gaps and conflicts on purpose.

## 1. Objectives

The objective of this code is to protect children who play learning games made by our members and to build trust with schools and parents.

## 2. Age ratings

1. Members shall display an age rating label and content descriptors prominently before download or purchase.
2. Age ratings shall consider violence, sexual content and gambling-like content.

## 3. Purchases and rewards

1. Members may offer randomised reward items purchasable with virtual currency, provided that the odds are disclosed.
2. Members should show the price of virtual currency in euro where possible.
3. Purchases by children under 13 must be approved by a parent or guardian.

## 4. Engagement

1. Push notifications may be enabled by default to remind players to return.
2. Members will not use countdown timers or false urgency to pressure children.
3. Daily streak rewards are allowed to encourage regular learning.

## 5. Communication and safety

1. Members must provide a report button and the ability to block other players in every chat.
2. Chat for players under 10 shall use predefined phrases only.
3. Members shall moderate chat using trained human moderators and automated filters.

## 6. Privacy

1. Members shall carry out a data protection impact assessment before launch.
2. Children's personal data will not be used for advertising or profiling.
3. Members shall publish a privacy notice in plain language that children can understand.

## 7. Artificial intelligence

1. AI chatbots must clearly disclose to children that they are not human.
2. Members may use conversation history to personalise AI tutors.

## 8. Compliance

Members will review this code every two years.
