# Default anti-bullying procedure

For schools that have no anti-bullying plan of their own. The product offers it at set-up (CORE-CHT-15).
The school accepts it, adapts it, or replaces it with its own plan. It is a starting point, not legal advice.
National child-protection law decides who must act in serious cases.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Does |
|---|---|---|
| First contact | The class teacher | Reads bullying reports for the class |
| School contact | The director, or a person the school names | Decides on actions and speaks to families |
| Safeguarding lead | Named in the safeguarding procedure (CORE-CHT-08) | Takes over serious cases |

## Steps

1. **Read the report** within one school day, with its evidence record.
2. **Make it safe in the product.** Hide the content. Keep the children apart in pairings and groups
   (CORE-CHT-12). Pause chat for the class if needed.
3. **Listen to the child who was targeted**, in private. Say it is not their fault. Ask what would help.
4. **Talk to the child who did it**, in private. Talk about the behaviour and how to repair it, not about
   who they are.
5. **Tell the families of both children** through the school, not through the product.
6. **Check again** with the child who was targeted after one week and after four weeks.
7. **Hand over to the safeguarding lead** if there are threats, sexual content, images or voices of the
   child, self-harm, or if it does not stop.
8. **Close the report** with a category and the actions taken. The child who reported gets the outcome
   (CORE-SAF-06), without details about the other child.

## Categories

From the EU common definition of cyberbullying: behaviour through digital technologies that humiliates,
excludes, abuses, harasses or harms, once or again and again.

| Category | Words for children |
|---|---|
| Humiliating | "Someone is making fun of me" |
| Excluding | "I'm being left out on purpose" |
| Abusing or harassing | "Someone is being mean to me or scaring me" |
| Harming | "Someone shared something about me" |
| Other | "Something else is wrong" |

The school sees totals per category for its classes, never figures that point to one child.

## Help outside the school

- Child helpline: 116 111 (across the EU).
- The national Safer Internet Centre helpline.
- Portugal: Linha Internet Segura 800 21 90 90 and SOS Criança 116 111. Schools can also use the national
  plan "Escola Sem Bullying. Escola Sem Violência".
