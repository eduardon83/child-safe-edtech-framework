CSEF Pack Builder

Define your organisation's own child-safety compliance pack for the Child-Safe EdTech Framework: minimum level, no-gos, forbidden SDKs and phrases, records you require, and your own rules. The pack is a YAML file the CSEF scanner applies on top of the public framework. Nothing leaves your browser until you publish.

1Your organisation

The pack id becomes the prefix of your rule ids (e.g. ACME-POL-01).

2Minimum compliance level

Every product using this pack is assessed at least at this level, even if its manifest asks for less.

3No-gos

Rules you never break. A no-go always applies and blocks the build at any level, even on medium-confidence signals.

4Forbidden packages and phrases

One per line. Packages are matched in package.json, requirements, Podfile, Gradle and pubspec. Phrases are matched case-insensitively in code and copy files.

5Records you require

Features that need a decision record (with a named approver) before release, linked in each product's csef.yaml.

6Your own rules

Requirements specific to your organisation. Add a regular expression if the scanner can detect a breach in code.

8Publish to GitHub

    
Opens GitHub's "new file" page in your repository with the pack filled in. You review and commit it there.
